# Backlog insights archive

This digest preserves constraints from the November 19, 2026 vision-aligned
reset. It is a provenance ledger, not a second active tracker or proof that the
referenced work is complete. Some sources remain open while native relationship
changes are pending. Active scope and horizons live on the surviving issues.

The [project plan](PROJECT_PLAN.md) and [issue workflow](ISSUE_MANAGEMENT.md)
govern promotion. The baseline was 176 open issues; the approved target is 33 (5
Now, 15 Next, 13 Later). No source is classified as completed by this reset.
Source issues retain their bodies and comments; rewritten survivors preserve
their previous bodies. The code review was anchored at
[`e232fd2`](https://github.com/fderuiter/cadence-clinical/commit/e232fd295b8a92a36260df33e50a99ede2996709).
[PR #5790](https://github.com/fderuiter/cadence-clinical/pull/5790) subsequently
advanced participant-portal recovery, draft, PIN, and sync behavior; those
changes do not prove the remaining real-host clinical handoffs or release
qualification. The execution refresh also checked `f63f336`: container and SCE
sandbox hardening (#5795, #5792), shared randomization helpers (#5793),
unreachable UI cleanup, and pooled gateway HMAC encoding (`267e285`). Retain
their shipped constraints and verify the remaining scope instead of redoing
them.

Billing/site finance, recruitment, downstream biostatistics, repository
extraction, and premature production rollout are outside the current scope.
Archived ideas require a new scope decision and current-code verification before
reuse. In particular, preserve attribution and account safeguards, separate
commercial entitlements from clinical authority, and retain intervention
provenance before reintroducing any adjacent capability.

Each entry records the specific signal, its destination, and links back to the
original source. The source-to-survivor matrix above also records checked code
anchors. Preserve exact reproductions, evidence URLs, screenshots, and approvals
in their source issues. Keep shortened canonical notes linked to the full
context, and preserve the untouched original body for rewritten survivors.
