# Cadence market hypotheses and evidence plan

**Status: hypotheses, not customer-validated market conclusions.** Competitors
are active in EDC, eTMF, CDISC, study building, medical-device research and
integrated suites. Claiming 'first', 'only', 'fully compliant', 'free' or 'FDA
approved' without evidence is prohibited.

## Hypotheses

- **Priority 1 — Protocol-to-EDC study build requires repeated manual
  interpretation:** _Possible differentiated workflow:_ USDM → governed
  EDC-agnostic spec → generated checks and UAT evidence → reproducible
  controlled release. _Evidence needed:_ Timed competitor workflow comparison
  and clinical data manager interviews.
- **Priority 1 — Site users repeat data entry and logins:** _Possible
  differentiated workflow:_ Task-based Workspace with authorized third-party
  integrations. _Evidence needed:_ Research coordinator time-on-task, handoff
  count and errors.
- **Priority 1 — Switching one vendor often forces broader procurement:**
  _Possible differentiated workflow:_ Complete standalone EDC/eTMF/eConsent
  products with published APIs. _Evidence needed:_ Sponsor/CRO procurement
  interviews and prototype integration tests.
- **Priority 2 — Software/device changes can obscure clinical evidence
  interpretation:** _Possible differentiated workflow:_
  Version/exposure-to-observation traceability for medical-device
  investigations. _Evidence needed:_ Medical-device investigator interviews and
  sample evidence review.
- **Priority 2 — Regulatory readiness depends on fragmented evidence:**
  _Possible differentiated workflow:_ Lineage from protocol, checks and dataset
  transformation to controlled submission artifacts. _Evidence needed:_
  Biostatistician/QA workflow validation.
- **Priority 3 — AI-generated content lacks reproducible review evidence:**
  _Possible differentiated workflow:_ Human-approved, attributed suggestions
  with generated tests and preserved versions. _Evidence needed:_ Safety/quality
  and workflow user studies.

## Competitive questions to research

Veeva, Medidata, Oracle Clinical One, Castor, OpenClinica, REDCap,
OpenStudyBuilder, CDISC CORE, Greenlight Guru and relevant specialist
eTMF/eConsent platforms. Compare documented _specific tasks_ and
interoperability/price/qualification requirements, not generalized screenshots
or vendor marketing.

## Discovery criteria

- Identify the daily user, economic buyer, implementation owner and compliance
  reviewer.
- Interview ~15–20 relevant prospects across small sponsors/CROs, research sites
  and medical-device studies (planning target, not achieved).
- Use consented task walkthroughs and synthetic study data. Record time,
  clicks/actions, duplicate transfers, failure modes and deployment
  requirements.
- Prioritize only hypotheses with repeated observable pain, compelling
  switching/buying reasons and a credible technical advantage.
- Do not publish market superiority claims from these hypotheses without
  measured comparative evidence.

## First demonstration

A synthetic medical-device USDM study. Show protocol amendment impact on
approved EDC spec, regenerated test cases, corrected source observations and
fully traceable analysis outputs. Treat both standalone and combined product
paths as acceptance scenarios.
