# Cadence market hypotheses and evidence plan

**Status: hypotheses, not customer-validated market conclusions.** Competitors are active in EDC, eTMF, CDISC, study building, medical-device research and integrated suites. Claiming 'first', 'only', 'fully compliant', 'free' or 'FDA approved' without evidence is prohibited.

## Hypotheses

| Priority | Customer pain                                                        | Possible differentiated workflow                                                                        | Evidence needed                                                           |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1        | Protocol-to-EDC study build requires repeated manual interpretation  | USDM → governed EDC-agnostic spec → generated checks and UAT evidence → reproducible controlled release | Timed competitor workflow comparison and clinical data manager interviews |
| 1        | Site users repeat data entry and logins                              | Task-based Workspace with authorized third-party integrations                                           | Research coordinator time-on-task, handoff count and errors               |
| 1        | Switching one vendor often forces broader procurement                | Complete standalone EDC/eTMF/eConsent products with published APIs                                      | Sponsor/CRO procurement interviews and prototype integration tests        |
| 2        | Software/device changes can obscure clinical evidence interpretation | Version/exposure-to-observation traceability for medical-device investigations                          | Medical-device investigator interviews and sample evidence review         |
| 2        | Regulatory readiness depends on fragmented evidence                  | Lineage from protocol, checks and dataset transformation to controlled submission artifacts             | Biostatistician/QA workflow validation                                    |
| 3        | AI-generated content lacks reproducible review evidence              | Human-approved, attributed suggestions with generated tests and preserved versions                      | Safety/quality and workflow user studies                                  |

## Competitive questions to research

Veeva, Medidata, Oracle Clinical One, Castor, OpenClinica, REDCap, OpenStudyBuilder, CDISC CORE, Greenlight Guru and relevant specialist eTMF/eConsent platforms. Compare documented _specific tasks_ and interoperability/price/qualification requirements, not generalized screenshots or vendor marketing.

## Discovery criteria

- Identify the daily user, economic buyer, implementation owner and compliance reviewer.
- Interview ~15–20 relevant prospects across small sponsors/CROs, research sites and medical-device studies (planning target, not achieved).
- Use consented task walkthroughs and synthetic study data. Record time, clicks/actions, duplicate transfers, failure modes and deployment requirements.
- Prioritize only hypotheses with repeated observable pain, compelling switching/buying reasons and a credible technical advantage.
- Do not publish market superiority claims from these hypotheses without measured comparative evidence.

## First demonstration

A synthetic medical-device USDM study. Show protocol amendment impact on approved EDC spec, regenerated test cases, corrected source observations and fully traceable analysis outputs. Treat both standalone and combined product paths as acceptance scenarios.
