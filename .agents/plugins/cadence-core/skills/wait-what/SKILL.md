# Wait-What: Reset & Re-Pitch

When the previous message missed the mark, was needlessly complex, or misunderstood the problem:

1. **Acknowledge and Stop**: Do not defend the previous response or double down on complexity.
2. **Strip the Noise**: Reframe the core problem in plain, direct language in 1-2 paragraphs.
3. **Ask a Direct Question**: Present a simple, concrete choice to realign.
