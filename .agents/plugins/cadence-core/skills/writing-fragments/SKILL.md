# Writing: Fragments

Exploratory brainstorming phase: capture raw fragments, quotes, observations, and code snippets quickly without forcing early categorization.

## Rules
- Focus on density and fidelity of observations.
- Keep fragments unconstrained by outline structure.
- Hand off to `writing-beats` or `writing-shape` when ready to structure.
