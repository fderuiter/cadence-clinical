# Support Policy: Cadence Clinical Research Software

Welcome to the **Cadence Clinical Research Software** support policy. This document outlines the proper channels for seeking support, reporting bugs, and inquiring about clinical evaluations or pilot testing.

---

## ⚠️ Pre-Demo Sandbox Warning & Constraints

The local containerized environment, or the **pre-demo sandbox**, is provided strictly for technical evaluation, development, and demonstration purposes. It is subject to the following constraints and limitations:

1. **Mock Data Only:** The sandbox is configured with default mock schemas and dummy patient data. It is **not** compliant with HIPAA or GDPR for actual patient data in its default local state.
2. **Non-Persistence:** Data stored in the local sandbox is ephemeral and should not be used for critical trial design or tracking outside of designated Docker volumes.
3. **No Production Use:** This environment is not qualified for GxP production workflows or official regulatory submissions.
4. **Evaluation Only:** Any clinical trials, pilot testing, or GxP evaluations require a qualified deployment configured and supported by our engineering team.

---

## 🚀 How to Contact Us

To ensure public stakeholders, clinical partners, and integrators are directed away from outdated legacy contact routes or premature GitHub issue logs, all inquiries are channeled to the correct domain.

- **Clinical Evaluations & Pilot Inquiries:**
  If you are a clinical partner evaluating the platform and want to inquire about pilot testing, official deployments, or production qualification, please do not expose these inquiries or sensitive questions on the public GitHub issue tracker. Instead, contact us directly at [ccrsoft.com](https://ccrsoft.com).
- **General Support & Business Inquiries:**
  For any business inquiries, partnership requests, general support, or integration assistance, please visit our central portal at [ccrsoft.com](https://ccrsoft.com).
- **Technical Bug Reports:**
  If you have identified a reproducible technical defect or bug in the open-source code base, you may log it on our GitHub issue tracker. Please ensure no PII/PHI or proprietary information is included in your report.

---

## 🚫 GitHub Issue Tracker Guidelines

To maintain an efficient and compliant development cycle, **zero non-technical support tickets or general inquiries should be opened in the public GitHub issues tracker**. 

Any tickets related to:
- Clinical trial design consultation
- Pilot testing access or setup requests
- Pricing or licensing questions
- General "how-to" questions for end-users

will be closed immediately and redirected to the official channels at [ccrsoft.com](https://ccrsoft.com).
